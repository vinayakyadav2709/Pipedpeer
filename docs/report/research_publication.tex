\section[PipedPeer: Running Unmodified Python Across a Small Group's Machines Without a Configured Cluster]{\raggedright PipedPeer: Running Unmodified Python Across a Small Group's Machines Without a Configured Cluster}

A journal article drawn from this work, ``PipedPeer: Running Unmodified Python
Across a Small Group's Machines Without a Configured Cluster'', by \candone,
\candtwo, \candthree\ and \guidename\ of the \department, \institute, was
submitted to the \emph{Journal of Parallel and Distributed Computing}
(Elsevier) as a regular research paper. Status: \publicationstatus.

The article presents PipedPeer for a systems readership and is organised as
follows.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Introduction} states the three obstacles to using nearby idle
  machines --- the environment, the code and the network --- and the article's
  contributions.
  \item \textbf{Background and related work} places PipedPeer against cluster
  frameworks for Python, volunteer computing, environment reproduction, NAT
  traversal, and communication-efficient data-parallel training.
  \item \textbf{Design overview} sets out the design goals and the architecture
  of Chapter~\ref{chap:arch}: one daemon per machine, coordination by the
  submitting machine, and an introducer in every daemon.
  \item \textbf{Mechanisms} and \textbf{Implementation} describe the parts of
  Chapter~\ref{chap:design}: content-addressed environments and path-level
  transfer, transparent interception with measured work sharing and the cost
  model, training synchronised through the daemon, and joining across networks.
  \item \textbf{Evaluation} states its questions and answers them with the
  measurements of Chapter~\ref{chap:results}, in the order of setup,
  microbenchmarks, end-to-end runs and training.
  \item \textbf{Discussion}, \textbf{Threats to validity and limitations} and
  \textbf{Conclusion and future work} close the article.
\end{itemize}

A shorter version in the IEEE conference format, ``\projecttitle'', six pages,
accompanies this report for its evaluation.
